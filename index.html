GigBounty Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your data is secure. To comply with Google Play Store policies and international data protection laws, please read our comprehensive privacy terms below.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google, we collect your Email Address, Display Name, and Profile Photo to create and manage your account.
  • Financial & Reward Data: We collect your UPI ID or wallet details purely to process withdrawal requests. We do not process direct bank payments or store credit card information.
  • Gameplay Data: We track your in-app currency (Coins, Pink Diamonds), task completions, and Leaderboard rankings.
  • Device & Technical Data: We collect device model, OS version, IP address, and crash logs to maintain app stability and prevent fraud.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the GigBounty platform.
  • To securely process your reward payouts and withdrawals.
  • To display public Leaderboards (only showing your Display Name and Avatar).
  • To analyze app usage and improve features, as well as detecting and preventing fraudulent activities.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Realtime Database & Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Crashlytics

4. Account & Data Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. We provide a transparent and easy way to delete your data, fully complying with Google Play's Data Deletion requirements.

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account at any time by navigating to the Profile Screen and tapping the "Delete Account" button.
  • Data Erasure: Upon requesting deletion, your account, email, profile data, earnings, and transaction history will be permanently and irreversibly erased from our active servers.
  • Web Deletion Request: You can also request data deletion by contacting our support email directly.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Services are strictly not intended for anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

6. Security of Your Data

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
