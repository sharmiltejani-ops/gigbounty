Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I earn money on GigBounty?

You can earn Coins and Pink Diamonds by completing simple tasks, reading articles, and sharing your opinions in surveys. Once you reach the minimum threshold, these can be converted into real cash.

2. How long does a withdrawal take?

Once you place a withdrawal request, our team manually reviews it to ensure all tasks were completed genuinely. This process takes up to 24 hours. Once approved, your money will be sent directly to your UPI ID.

3. Why was my account banned?

If your account was banned, it means our automated security system detected fraudulent activity. This includes (but is not limited to) using a VPN, creating multiple accounts on the same device, or using fake details to complete advertiser surveys. Banned accounts are permanent and cannot be recovered.

4. Is the app completely free?

Yes, GigBounty is 100% free to use. We will never ask you to pay money or make any investment to use our platform or withdraw your earnings.

5. I completed a task but didn't get points. Why?

Sometimes it takes a few minutes for third-party advertisers to verify the task completion. Please ensure you followed all the instructions perfectly. If you still didn't get points after 24 hours, it means the advertiser rejected the completion due to invalid or missing steps.
