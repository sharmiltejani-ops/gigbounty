GigBounty Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your data is secure. To comply with Google Play Store policies and applicable Indian data protection laws, please read our privacy terms below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The information we collect includes:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google, we collect your Email Address, Display Name, and Profile Photo to create and manage your account.
  • Financial & Reward Data: We collect your UPI ID or wallet details purely to process withdrawal requests. We do not process direct bank payments or store credit card information.
  • Gameplay Data: We track your in-app currency (Coins, Pink Diamonds), task completions, and Leaderboard rankings.
  • Device & Technical Data: We collect device model, OS version, IP address, and crash logs to maintain app stability and prevent fraud.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the GigBounty platform.
  • To securely process your reward payouts and withdrawals. Withdrawal requests are currently reviewed and processed manually by our team using the UPI ID/wallet details you provide — we do not currently route these transactions through an automated third-party payment gateway.
  • To display public Leaderboards (only showing your Display Name and Avatar).
  • To analyze app usage and improve features, as well as detecting and preventing fraudulent activities.

3. Data Sharing and Selling of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for money or any other consideration. Your data is only shared with the service providers listed in Section 4 (to operate the App) or when required by law.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Realtime Database & Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Crashlytics

5. Advertising

GigBounty does not currently display third-party advertisements. If we introduce in-app advertising in the future (for example, through Google AdMob), we will update this Privacy Policy in advance to disclose the ad networks used, any Advertising ID or related data collected for ad personalization, and how you can manage your ad preferences.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and activity data for as long as your account remains active. When you request account deletion, we review and process the request within 15 days, after which your data is permanently and irreversibly erased from our active servers, except where we are legally required to retain certain records.

7. Account & Data Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. We provide a transparent and easy way to delete your data, in line with Google Play's Data Deletion requirements.

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account at any time by navigating to the Profile Screen and tapping the "Delete Account" button.
  • Web Deletion Request: You can also request account and data deletion without using the app by visiting: https://gigbounty.online/delete-account (replace with your live deletion page or a Google Form link — this exact URL also needs to go into the Data Safety form in Play Console).
  • Data Erasure: Upon requesting deletion, your account, email, profile data, earnings, and transaction history will be permanently and irreversibly erased from our active servers.

8. Your Rights (India)

GigBounty is currently offered to users in India, and your data is handled in accordance with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as its provisions come into force. You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated data.
  • Withdraw consent and request erasure of your data (see Section 7).
  • Raise a grievance about how your data is collected or used.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 12.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Services are strictly not intended for anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

10. Security of Your Data

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or app features (such as the introduction of advertising). We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page whenever we make changes, and where the change is significant, we will also notify you through an in-app notice. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

12. Contact Us

GigBounty is currently developed and operated by an individual developer based in India. If you have any questions, concerns, or require assistance with data deletion or exercising your rights, please contact:

Email: support@gigbounty.online
