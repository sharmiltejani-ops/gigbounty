GigBounty Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal data is handled responsibly and securely. To comply with Google Play Developer policies, ad network regulations, and applicable Indian data protection laws (including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), please review our complete privacy terms below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, and protect our platform and reward services. The information we collect includes:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In (via Google Credential Manager), we collect your Email Address, Display Name, Profile Photo URL, and Google User Identifier (UID) to create and authenticate your account.
  • Redemption & Payout Information: When you submit a withdrawal or gift card redemption request, we collect:
    • UPI ID: For direct cash transfers via UPI (Unified Payments Interface).
    • Delivery Email Address: For digital gift cards and voucher codes (Google Play Store Redeem Codes and Amazon Gift Cards) to deliver the voucher code directly to your inbox.
    We strictly do not collect or store bank account numbers, debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, or net banking passwords.
  • Task, Earning & Reward Data: We record your in-app balances (Coins, Pink Diamonds), task completions (including Math Quizzes, Word Puzzles, Surveys, Bumper giveaway participation, and Daily Check-ins/Streaks), transaction history, and Leaderboard rankings.
  • Referral Program Data: We generate a unique referral code linked to your account and track successful invite completions to award bonus reward coins.
  • Device Integrity & Anti-Fraud Data: To protect the app from bots, emulator automation, multiple account abuse, and artificial traffic, we collect technical data such as device model, OS version, IP address, screen resolution, active session tokens, and detect the presence of VPNs, proxies, and developer debugging environments.
  • Advertising Data: Our advertising partners—Google AdMob and Unity Ads (via AdMob mediation and real-time bidding)—may collect your resettable Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, device specifications, and ad interaction metrics to deliver and measure ad performance. See Section 5 for full details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate application purposes:

  • Platform Operation: To authenticate your login, maintain user balances, and enable gameplay and task activities.
  • Payout & Reward Fulfillment: To review, process, and fulfill your reward requests—transferring cash amounts to your provided UPI ID or sending digital codes (Google Play Redeem Codes and Amazon Gift Cards) to your delivery email address.
  • Community & Leaderboards: To display top earners on the public Leaderboard (showing only your Display Name, Avatar, and earned Coins).
  • Anti-Fraud & Account Security: To prevent illegitimate multi-accounting, VPN spoofing, click fraud, and automated emulator attacks, ensuring fair earning opportunities for all genuine users.
  • Serving In-App Advertisements: To deliver certified video, interstitial, and native ad experiences through Google AdMob and Unity Ads, which fund the platform and allow users to earn in-app rewards.

3. Data Sharing and Selling of Information

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to any data brokers or commercial third parties. Your data is only shared with verified backend infrastructure and advertising partners specified in Section 4 solely to operate the app, or if required under applicable Indian legal processes.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

GigBounty integrates industry-standard third-party SDKs to provide secure authentication, database hosting, crash reporting, and ads:

  • Google Play Services & Credential Manager: User authentication and Google Sign-In.
  • Firebase Authentication: Secure credential management.
  • Firebase Realtime Database & Cloud Firestore: Cloud database storage for profiles, balances, and reward history.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Dynamic configuration and app feature updates.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics: Performance monitoring, crash diagnosis, and app stability.
  • Google AdMob: Primary mobile advertising platform and mediation manager.
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): Real-time bidding mediation advertising partner.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob & Unity Ads Mediation)

GigBounty utilizes Google AdMob and certified mediation bidding partners, including Unity Ads, to display standard ad formats in compliance with Google Play Developer policies:

  • Rewarded Video Ads: User-initiated ads that you voluntarily choose to watch to earn Coins, Pink Diamonds, or bonus attempts.
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen ads displayed at natural break points (such as between task questions or when accessing your transaction records), featuring clear close buttons and countdown timers without interrupting active gameplay.
  • Native Ads: Contextual ad cards seamlessly styled within task and quiz screens.

Our advertising partners may collect device model, advertising identifier (GAID), IP address, and ad interaction metrics to deliver contextual or personalized ads. For full privacy details, visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from AdMob
  • Unity Technologies Privacy Policy (Unity Ads)
  • Unity Ads Data & Privacy Overview

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via:

  • Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete Advertising ID (Android 12+) or toggle "Opt out of Ads Personalization".
  • Visiting Google Ads Settings.

6. Platform Integrity & Anti-Fraud Controls

To ensure a fair reward ecosystem for all genuine users and prevent exploitation:

  • VPN & Proxy Prevention: GigBounty detects and blocks VPNs, proxies, and IP-masking tools. Using VPNs or proxy networks is strictly prohibited and may result in withdrawal rejection or account suspension.
  • Emulator & Debugging Detection: The app restricts execution in emulators, virtual sandbox environments, or devices with active USB debugging to prevent automated script abuse.
  • Single Session Control: Each account is tied to an active session token. Simultaneous logins on multiple devices are automatically terminated to safeguard your coin balance.

7. External Links & Community Channels (Telegram)

GigBounty offers links to our official public community channel on Telegram (@GigbountyEarnMoney) for updates and tips:

  • Independent Platform: Telegram is operated independently by Telegram FZ-LLC, governed by the Telegram Privacy Policy.
  • No In-App Credentials Shared: Clicking the Telegram link opens your external browser or Telegram app. No GigBounty login credentials or personal data are shared.
  • Safety Reminder: Participation is voluntary. Never share sensitive information (such as passwords, OTPs, or UPI PINs) in public channels. Official support will never ask for your passwords or PINs.

8. Data Retention

We retain your profile and reward activity while your account is active. When you initiate an account deletion request, your data is processed and permanently wiped from our active databases within 7 business days, except where statutory Indian financial or legal retention applies.

9. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You have complete authority over your data. In full compliance with Google Play Data Deletion requirements, you may delete your account and all associated data at any time through:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to the Profile screen inside GigBounty and tap "Delete Account". Your account authentication, profile, balances, task records, and withdrawal entries will be permanently deleted.
  • Web Deletion Request: You can submit a deletion request without installing or opening the app by visiting our dedicated deletion portal: https://gigbounty.online/delete-account.html
  • Scope of Erasure: Upon deletion, your account UID, Google display credentials, earnings history, and reward records are permanently and irreversibly purged from our servers.

10. Your Rights (India)

GigBounty is offered exclusively to users in India. We operate in adherence to the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). You have the right to:

  • Access and review the personal data stored with your account.
  • Request rectification of inaccurate data.
  • Withdraw consent and request complete erasure of your account and personal data.
  • Lodge an inquiry or grievance regarding data processing practices.

11. Children's Privacy (Strictly 18+)

GigBounty is strictly intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. Minors under 18 years old are strictly prohibited from using the platform. We do not knowingly solicit or collect information from minors. If we identify that an account belongs to anyone under 18, we will immediately terminate the account and permanently delete all related records.

12. Security of Your Data

We employ industry-standard encryption, Firebase security rules, and secure HTTPS/TLS transmission protocols to safeguard your personal details. While we take comprehensive precautions, no electronic transmission or cloud storage system is 100% impenetrable, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements, partner policy updates, or regulatory shifts. The "Last Updated" date will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of GigBounty following any updates constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
